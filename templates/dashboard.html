OPTION 1

📄 Batch Scan — CSV Dataset

  • 1 Prepare a CSV with the 16 required voice feature columns — MDVP:Fo(Hz), Jitter, Shimmer, HNR, NHR and others.
  • 2 Each row is one recording. Include a name column to group multiple recordings per person.
  • 3 Click Upload & Predict — the DBN model analyses every person and shows risk results.

💡 Test with parkinsons.csv (UCI dataset, 195 recordings).

OPTION 2

🎤 AI Voice Scan — Upload WAV

  • 1 Upload a WAV audio file containing voice (e.g. sustained vowel or short speech).
  • 2 The system extracts 16 acoustic biomarkers (pitch, jitter, shimmer, HNR, NHR) from your voice using Praat analysis.
  • 3 You get a risk level (Low / Medium / High) and a downloadable report with biomarker values.

Processing pipeline:

📥 Uploading file
🎵 Extracting pitch tracks (Praat)
📐 Computing jitter & shimmer
🔢 Computing HNR · NHR
🧠 Running model
📊 Generating report
💡 Tips for best accuracy
• Use a WAV file with clear voice (sustained vowel or short speech, 4+ seconds).
• Use a WAV file recorded in a quiet environment; avoid background music or fan noise.
• Supported: .wav (other formats may be converted by the server if supported).
